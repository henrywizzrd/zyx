## ZYX v1.2.0

### New
- **Pop style.** A second look next to the glass one: flat colours, bold outlines and hard shadows across the whole app, with its own colour themes. Switch any time in Settings › Appearance.
- **Welcome screens.** A new sign-in poster, then pick the genres you like, see what's inside, choose your look and drop the needle to start.
- **Your genres start the queue.** Suggestions begin with the genres you picked and follow your own taste as you listen.
- **Import from Spotify.** Paste a public Spotify playlist or album link (Library › + or Settings › Import playlists from) and ZYX rebuilds it with the closest match for every song.
- **Landscape player.** Turn your phone sideways for a flat tile layout: big cover, lyrics, player buttons and volume side by side.
- **Trending by country.** Trending starts on your phone's country, with a flag and a country picker.
- **Support ZYX.** Settings › Support ZYX, by VietQR in Vietnam or PayPal worldwide. Nothing is locked behind it.

### Better
- Theme Studio shows every theme as a compact tile; Light, Ocean, Latte, Snow and Sakura are easier to read.
- Frosted glass is the new default glass type.
- Settings are grouped into clear sections: Appearance, Playback, Import, Account and Help.
- Smoother player, queue and lyrics with fewer redraws.
- "Send diagnostics" switch (on by default, can be turned off): a daily summary with counts only, no account details; the full app log is sent only for a day with a crash or a freeze.

### Removed
- Rain and Neon colour themes (the Neon glass type stays), and the Smoked glass type.
